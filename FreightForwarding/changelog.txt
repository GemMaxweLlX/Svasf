Version: 2.0.0
Date: 7 November 2026
  Major Features:
    - Updated to Factorio 2.1
    - Optional Space Age support: Nauvis keeps the Freight Forwarding island terrain and resources, other planets are unaffected
  Space Age:
    - Startup setting "Classic mode": Freight Forwarding exactly as in 1.x on Nauvis even with Space Age (lava pools,
      cobalt and titansteel on Nauvis, one rocket silo type that needs a rocket silo hole on Nauvis, rocket parts with
      titansteel frames and battery packs); other planets stay as in vanilla Space Age. Everything below is off then
    - Tiered rockets: the rocket silo technology unlocks the light rocket silo (500 kg lift, 20 rocket parts, cheaper),
      the light space platform starter pack (500 kg) and the small cargo landing pad (20 slots)
    - On Nauvis the light rocket silo can only be built exactly on a rocket silo hole (on other planets anywhere); one
      hole is guaranteed on the second ring of islands (about 2000-2700 tiles from spawn)
    - The normal rocket silo, landing pad and starter pack are unlocked by "Heavy-lift rockets", available in three
      versions using the science of Vulcanus, Fulgora or Gleba: researching one completes the others. The normal silo
      can be built anywhere
    - Each of these planets has its own rocket silo recipe from local materials (tungsten / superconductors and
      holmium / carbon fiber), craftable only on that planet
    - Heavy rocket silo (2000 kg) unlocked with cryogenic science and titansteel: its rocket parts use titansteel rocket
      frames. Normal silos can be upgraded to it
    - Lava pools are generated on Vulcanus (500-1200 tiles from the landing site) instead of Nauvis, so titansteel is
      smelted there. Titansteel processing needs metallurgic science
    - Cobalt comes from cobalt vents in the lava lakes of Vulcanus, mined with the dredger (cobalt crust can only be
      dredged on Vulcanus, seabed nodules only on Nauvis). Cobalt processing needs metallurgic science
    - Batteries are fuel tiers that burn into empty batteries and are charged again on any planet:
      1. lead-acid battery (4 MJ, anywhere), 2. cobalt-holmium battery pack (10 MJ, made on Fulgora, discharges after
      1 hour; needs electromagnetic science), 3. lithium battery pack (25 MJ, faster, holds its charge, made on Aquilo)
    - Refrigerated containers (with Intermodal Containers): made on Gleba, carry spoilable items (Gleba produce,
      nutrients, bioflux, bacteria, science, fish, charged battery packs) which spoil 5 times slower inside
    - Light containers (Vulcanus): any containerized item, a full one always weighs 250 kg - 4 per rocket
    - Cryo containers (Aquilo): spoilable items spoil 20 times slower, 250 kg when full
    - Interplanetary freight science pack, assembled on Aquilo from transport science, titansteel, a charged battery
      pack, a refrigerated container and lithium; needed by all technologies that use cryogenic science
    - Fast cargo ships (Vulcanus, 1.5x speed) and heavy cargo ships (Aquilo, double hold), registered through
      Cargo Ships' ship definition API
    - Lava barges (Vulcanus): waterways can be built on lava (rails and rail supports still can't); other ships burn
      on lava
    - Train tiers: locomotive Mk2 (Fulgora) and Mk3 (Aquilo), cargo and fluid wagon Mk2 with double capacity (Vulcanus)
    - Icebreaker (Aquilo): turns the ice around it into brash ice, so waterways can be built further
    - Spoilage sensor: a combinator next to a train stop that outputs the spoilage of the stopped train's cargo
      (most spoiled stack and average, in percent) for circuit wait conditions
    - Rail supports can't stand in deep water (like on Fulgora's deep oil ocean): elevated rails only bridge
      shallow straits, the open ocean still needs ships
    - Fulgora: scrap seamounts on the deep oil ocean floor, dredged for scrap
    - Aquilo: fluorine seeps in the ammoniacal ocean, pumped by the oil rig
    - Gleba: ships crawl through wetlands at a quarter of their speed; swamp barges don't
    - Hot titansteel spoils into deformed plates after 2 minutes; the foundry melts those back into steel and
      titanium
    - Dredgers keep their quality (faster with higher quality)
    - Dredger Mk2 (Vulcanus: 6x6, speed 2, 2 module slots) and Mk3 (Aquilo: 8x8, speed 3, 4 module slots)
      with bigger platforms (24x24 and 30x30); platforms are no longer laid over waterways, rails, ships or trains
    - Balance (with startup settings): light/cryo containers 400 kg, fluorine seeps 35% yield and half as common,
      60-140 scrap per dredging cycle, no productivity modules in dredgers, ships at 40% speed in Gleba wetlands,
      heavy cargo ship 0.7x speed, wagon Mk2 1.5x capacity, no transport science in technologies that need another
      planet's science
    - Spoiled refrigerated/cryo containers now hold what their contents spoil into, in the same amount (e.g. 500
      yumako -> 500 spoilage, bacteria -> ore), and give back the same kind of container (a cryo container used
      to come back as a refrigerated one, with a fixed 100 spoilage)
    - Containers wear out: when unloading, 1% are lost and 2% come back damaged (all container kinds). Damaged
      containers are repaired by hand for a quarter of their materials or recycled (classic mode: 1% loss as in
      Intermodal Containers)
    - Recipe tabs: separate packing and unpacking tabs for normal, refrigerated, light and cryo containers; all
      filled containers in the container tab
    - Icons of tiered things show the tier number (top right) and the planet where they are made (bottom left)
    - Tips and tricks for the Space Age features
    - Interstellar freight contract: pack freight consignments from goods of every planet and deliver them to a
      space platform at the solar system edge to win (contract size is a startup setting, 100 by default); Space Age's
      own victory (just reaching the edge) is replaced by it
    - Contract choice: the caravan offers 3 of 6 contract types, each with its own size and reward (robot speed and
      braking, mining productivity, research speed, inserter capacity, robot cargo, manual crafting). Nothing is
      taken until a contract is accepted; it can be changed until the first delivery. Contracts keep coming and
      grow by 50% of the first each time
    - Contract timers (map settings): offers expire after 30 minutes (±25%), all offers are refreshed every 45
      minutes, an accepted contract has 180 minutes per 100 consignments; when overdue, the contract grows by 5%
      every 10 minutes until fulfilled. Timers are shown in the freight panel
    - Lore for the contracts: the Long Caravan, interstellar haulers that take cargo on the fly at the solar system
      edge (descriptions, tips, messages, victory screen)
    - Delivery permits (exclusive to FF + Space Age, startup setting): 11 key technologies first need a permit,
      which can't be researched in labs. When it becomes available, a research post appears at a random place on land
      near the explored area of the given planet; delivering the containers it asks for (used up) grants the permit.
      Saves where the key technology is already researched get the permit automatically. The research post is found
      damaged and is restored by delivering repair materials into it (steel, circuits and a local material per
      planet); after the delivery a small rocket takes off with the records, and 5
      minutes later the post blows itself up
    - Contract reputation: +1 for a contract on time (+2 for a special order), -1 when late; rare ships (flagship,
      forge ship, observatory ship) from reputation 3, bigger contracts with a bad reputation
    - Special orders: specific goods in light or cryo containers instead of consignments, double reward
    - Fulgora: lightning storms at night slow ships to half speed and can strike moving ships away from lightning rods
    - Sea infrastructure (all games except classic mode): port crane (long-reach bulk inserter), cable terminal
      (64-tile wires between terminals), offshore wind turbine (up to 1 MW with the wind), tidal generator (up to
      600 kW with the tide)
    - Subsea cable: cable landings on two shores; open one, choose the other landing and lay the cable (1 subsea
      cable per tile, 2 tiles per second, up to 1000 tiles). When laid it is a normal copper wire. With Space Age, cable
      landings can only be built on Nauvis and Gleba, and each lays its own cable, made only there: subsea cable
      (Nauvis: copper cable, lead, plastic) and swamp cable (Gleba: copper cable, carbon fiber, spoilage)
    - Ship scripts (lava, wetlands, icebreaker, storms) find ships through the train manager instead of searching
      whole surfaces
    - Translations of the new texts: Ukrainian, French, German, Japanese
    - Message for saves from Freight Forwarding 1.x
    - Two panels on the left, toggled by shortcut bar buttons: containers (freight statistics per planet: packed,
      unpacked, damaged; refreshed every 10 seconds) and contracts (offers, active contract, timers, rewards). The
      contracts button becomes available with the contract technology, which also opens the panel once
    - FF buildings can't be built on space platforms; the underwater pipe doesn't need heating on Aquilo
    - Light silos and small landing pads can be upgraded in place (upgrade planner)
    - Titansteel no longer blocks the first rocket: rocket parts stay vanilla, rocket frames are unlocked with titansteel
  Quality of life:
    - Fuel depot: trains and ships stopped within 10 tiles refuel from it and drop their burnt fuel into it
    - Map tags for rocket silo holes, cobalt vents and big lava pools when they are charted (setting; remote
      interface add_map_tags to tag an existing map)
    - The dredger can be mined and moved: the seamount/vent and the original tiles are restored
    - Space Age: containerization machine tier 4 (Fulgora), dredging productivity 1-3 (Vulcanus), fast charging
      station (Fulgora), seismic scanner and sonar buoy Mk2 with twice the range (Aquilo)
  Changes:
    - Map generation rewritten for the 2.x string noise expressions (island elevation, resource placement, enemy spread, water tiles). The generated world follows the same formulas as 1.x
    - The "Freight Forwarding" map preset now uses the "water" autoplace control (Scale / Coverage) instead of the removed terrain segmentation / water settings
    - Added startup setting "Island terrain by default" (on by default): Nauvis uses the island terrain even with the Default preset
    - Cliffs follow the Freight Forwarding elevation
    - Rocket control units no longer exist: rockets parts need 5 charged battery packs instead (not with Space Age)
    - With Space Age: satellite/space science and the interstellar communicator endgame are disabled (Space Age has its own victory)
    - Mini Trains, Spidertron Patrols, Lex's Aircraft, Hovercrafts, Canal Builder and Beautiful Bridge Railway are now optional dependencies
    - Intermodal Containers is optional for now: its latest release (2.1.3) only supports Factorio 2.0
    - Requires Cargo Ships Oil Rig (oil rigs were split out of Cargo Ships in 2.1)
    - Water-object collisions now apply to Cargo Ships' ship (un)loading pumps, which replaced pumps on water in 2.1
    - Freight Forwarding main menu simulations are disabled until they are re-recorded in 2.x
  Modding:
    - Prototypes are defined through prototypes/legacy.lua, which converts the 1.1 format (recipes, flags, hr_version, emissions, fluid boxes, collision masks, graphics sets) to 2.x
    - Custom collision layers are now collision-layer prototypes: ff_non_deep_water, ff_deep_water, ff_platform, ff_water_object, ff_substitute_player, ff_underwater_pipe, ff_lava_pool, ff_lava_pool_water_tile
    - Recipe/technology helpers no longer depend on bzlead's data-util
    - Handles 2.1 renames: recipe categories, item fuel_categories, product independent_probability
    - Dredger graphics are now taken from the vanilla pumpjack prototype

    - Added dredging platforms, which can be placed on seamounts to dredge for polymetallic nodules
      - Dredging platforms have a fixed size
      - Underground belts and electric poles cannot be placed on dredging platforms so they should be powered by charged batteries
